Questions, answered

Anything else, write to the instructor or to us from the footer.

Can I watch before I pay?
Yes. Instructors can mark any lecture as a free preview, and it plays on the course page without an account. The rest of the course stays locked until you enroll.
How do I pay for a course?
Through Razorpay Checkout, which opens on the course page. We verify the payment on our server before enrolling you, and a webhook backs that up if your connection drops. Free courses skip payment entirely.
What if I click Buy twice?
You can't buy a course you already own. Once you are enrolled, the button reads Start course and takes you straight to the player.
How do I start teaching?
Choose Instructor when you sign up. You get an instructor dashboard where you create a course, upload lectures, mark free previews and publish when it's ready.
What can I upload?
Lecture videos up to 500 MB, uploaded straight from your browser, or a YouTube link. Course thumbnails can be JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB.
Can someone share my lecture links?
Uploaded videos are served through signed links that expire after an hour, and only to enrolled students, the course's instructor and admins.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, from your profile. It removes your account along with your orders and enrollments, and any courses you created.
